The Story So Far

History of Space Exploration

Seven decades of launches, landings, and leaps of faith — the milestones that pushed humanity beyond its home planet.

Four Eras

The Ages of Spaceflight

The Space Race
1957 – 1969

A fierce Cold War competition between the USA and Soviet Union that produced the first satellites, the first human in space, and the Moon landings.

The Shuttle Era
1972 – 2011

The Space Shuttle made space routine — deploying telescopes, building the ISS, and flying 135 missions.

The ISS Era
1998 – Present

International cooperation produced the largest structure ever assembled in space.

The Commercial Era
2010 – Present

Private companies like SpaceX transformed spaceflight with reusable rockets.
