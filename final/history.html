The Story So Far

History of Space Exploration

Seven decades of launches, landings, and leaps of faith — the milestones that pushed humanity beyond its home planet.

Four Eras

The Ages of Spaceflight

The Space Race
1957 – 1969

Cold War competition that produced first satellites and Moon landing.

The Shuttle Era
1972 – 2011

Reusable spacecraft era that built the ISS and flew 135 missions.

The ISS Era
1998 – Present

International cooperation creating the largest structure in space.

The Commercial Era
2010 – Present

Private companies revolutionizing space with reusable rockets.

Key Milestones

Mission Timeline

1957
Sputnik 1 — First Satellite

First artificial satellite launched by the Soviet Union.

1961
Vostok 1 — First Human in Space

Yuri Gagarin becomes the first human in orbit.

1969
Apollo 11 — Moon Landing

First humans walk on the Moon.

1977
Voyager Missions

Exploration of outer planets and interstellar space.

1990
Hubble Telescope

Revolutionized deep space astronomy.

2020
Crew Dragon

Return of human launches from US soil.

2021
James Webb Telescope

Deepest view of the universe ever captured.
