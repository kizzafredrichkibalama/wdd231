The Story So Far

History of Space Exploration

Seven decades of launches, landings, and leaps of faith — the milestones that pushed humanity beyond its home planet.

Four Eras

The Ages of Spaceflight

The Space Race
1957 – 1969

A fierce Cold War competition between the USA and Soviet Union that produced the first satellites, the first human in space, and ultimately the Moon landings.

The Shuttle Era
1972 – 2011

The Space Shuttle made space feel routine — deploying telescopes, building the ISS, and flying 135 missions.

The ISS Era
1998 – Present

International cooperation produced the largest structure ever assembled in space, continuously inhabited for over two decades.

The Commercial Era
2010 – Present

Private companies like SpaceX disrupted the launch industry with reusable rockets, driving costs down and opening space to new missions.

Key Milestones

Mission Timeline

1957
Sputnik 1

The world's first artificial satellite launches, inaugurating the Space Age.

1961
Vostok 1

Yuri Gagarin becomes the first human in space.

1969
Apollo 11

Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin become the first humans to walk on the Moon.

1977
Voyager

Twin probes launch to explore the outer solar system, eventually reaching interstellar space.

1981
Space Shuttle

Columbia's first flight marks the era of reusable spaceflight.

1998
ISS Construction

The first module of the International Space Station launches.

2020
Crew Dragon

SpaceX's Crew Dragon carries NASA astronauts to the ISS from US soil for the first time since 2011.

2021
JWST & Ingenuity

The James Webb Space Telescope launches, and Ingenuity becomes the first helicopter to fly on Mars.

2023+
Starship Era

SpaceX's fully reusable Starship begins testing, designed to carry humans to Mars.
