01

Site Name

Beyond Earth

The name Beyond Earth captures the core mission of the site: exploring the machines that take humanity past the boundaries of our planet. It is evocative, memorable, and works equally well as a brand name and a conceptual statement about space exploration.

Optional domain suggestion:

beyond-earth.space

The .space TLD reinforces the subject matter and strengthens brand identity.

02

Site Purpose

Beyond Earth is a three-page educational encyclopedia dedicated to spacecraft — the vehicles engineered to travel, operate, or survive in outer space. The site will provide:

  • An overview of major spacecraft types (crewed capsules, probes, rovers, telescopes, stations)
  • A historical timeline of landmark spacecraft missions from Sputnik to present day
  • A forward-looking page covering next-generation spacecraft and upcoming missions

The target audience is students, space enthusiasts, and curious readers who want reliable, visually engaging information about humanity's most ambitious machines.

Informational Educational 3 Pages No Login Required

04

Color Scheme

The palette draws from deep space imagery — the void of space, starlight, and the glow of rocket engines. All colors are defined as CSS custom properties in styles.css.

#060C1A — Deep Space Primary page background; creates the void-of-space atmosphere.
#0D1B35 — Nebula Dark Card and section surfaces; subtle separation from background.
#00C8FF — Cyan Thrust Headings, links, accents, borders, and interactive highlights.
#F97316 — Engine Glow Call-to-action elements, labels, and secondary accents.
#CDD8F0 — Starlight Body copy and paragraph text for comfortable readability.
05

Typography

Font 1 — Display / Headings

Orbitron

Used for: site name, page titles (h1, h2), section headers. Geometric and futuristic — evokes spacecraft engineering aesthetics.

Font 2 — Labels / Code / UI

Share Tech Mono

Used for: data labels, mission stats, navigation tags, captions. Monospace feel reinforces a technical and engineering context.

Font 3 — Body / Paragraphs

Barlow — The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. Spacecraft are the pinnacle of human engineering ambition.

Used for: all body copy and descriptive paragraphs. Highly legible at all sizes; clean and unobtrusive alongside the display fonts.

All three fonts are loaded via Google Fonts and applied through CSS custom properties in styles.css.
